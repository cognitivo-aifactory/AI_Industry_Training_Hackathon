# AFR news corpus

The Australian Financial Review (AFR) full-text news corpus is not distributed through this public repository.

The corpus was removed because the articles may be subject to third-party copyright and may contain personal information. Do not commit, upload, fork, mirror, or otherwise redistribute copies of the corpus.

## Requesting access

Participants who need the corpus for an approved event activity should contact the event organizers. Each request will be reviewed, and access will only be provided through an approved private method where licensing, privacy, and event requirements permit it.

Access is not automatic, and receiving access does not grant permission to republish or redistribute the corpus.

## Repository-history decision

Decision recorded 24 August 2026: purge the removed AFR files from Git history after coordinating the rewrite with maintainers of open pull requests and public forks.

A normal deletion from the default branch does not remove the files from earlier commits. The history rewrite is therefore required to address the copyright and personal-information exposure. Until that coordinated rewrite is complete, do not use or share historical copies of the AFR files.
